# Patristic Researcher Resources

This folder is for **user-downloaded content** that supplements the committed index files.

## Why This Folder Exists

The `files/` directory contains our created indexes and references, which are small and committed to git.

This `resources/` folder is gitignored and holds larger public domain texts you download locally for richer research.

## Recommended Resources

### Ante-Nicene Fathers (ANF)

The 10-volume collection edited by Roberts and Donaldson:

- **Source**: [CCEL ANF Collection](https://ccel.org/ccel/schaff/anf01)
- **Alternative**: [New Advent Fathers](https://www.newadvent.org/fathers/)
- **Format**: HTML, PDF, or ePub

### Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers (NPNF)

Two series edited by Philip Schaff:
- **Series 1** (14 volumes): Augustine, Chrysostom
- **Series 2** (14 volumes): Eusebius, Athanasius, Jerome, Cappadocians

- **Source**: [CCEL NPNF Collection](https://ccel.org/ccel/schaff/npnf101)
- **Alternative**: [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/search?query=nicene+post-nicene+fathers)
