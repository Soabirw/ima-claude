# Index: Disputed Questions (Quaestiones Disputatae)

## Purpose

The **Quaestiones Disputatae** ("Disputed Questions") are records of Thomas' academic disputations—formal university debates where a master defended positions on philosophical and theological topics. These works often provide Thomas' most detailed and philosophically sophisticated treatment of individual topics.

Unlike the *Summa*, which surveys everything systematically, the Disputed Questions drill deep into particular problems.

### Key Questions in Detail

#### Q. 1: On Truth (*De Veritate*)

**The foundational question on truth itself**

| Article | Question | Key Teaching |
|---------|----------|--------------|
| 1 | What is truth? | "Truth is the conformity of thing and intellect" |
| 2 | Is truth more in intellect or things? | Principally in intellect |
| 3 | Is truth only in the composing/dividing intellect? | Properly, yes |
| 4 | Is there one or many truths? | Many created truths; one uncreated Truth |
| 5 | Is created truth eternal? | Only in God |
| 8 | Is every truth from the first truth? | Yes, as from exemplar cause |
| 9 | Is truth in sense? | Not properly, but by extension |
| 10 | Is anything false? | Only in relation to intellect |
| 12 | Are truth and being convertible? | Yes |

**Key passage** (DV 1.1 corp.):
> "Truth is defined as the conformity of intellect and thing. Hence to know this conformity is to know truth."

**Key passage** (DV 1.1 ad 1):
> "When it is said that 'truth is the conformity of thing and intellect,' this can be understood in two ways... The conformity can be considered according as the thing conforms to the intellect, and thus we have the truth of a thing; or according as the intellect conforms to the thing, and this is the truth of knowledge."

#### Q. 2: God's Knowledge

| Article | Question | Key Content |

| 3 | Is God's knowledge the cause of things? | Yes |
| 5 | Does God know singulars? | Yes |
| 12 | Does God know future contingents? | Yes—infallibly |
| 14 | Does God know infinite things? | Yes |

**Key passage** (DV 2.12 corp.):
> "God knows future contingents not in themselves... but in their cause, which is the divine will."

### Key Questions in Detail

#### Q. 1: The Nature of Evil

| Article | Question | Key Teaching |
|---------|----------|--------------|
| 1 | Is evil something? | No—it is a privation, not a thing |
| 2 | Is evil found in good? | Yes—only in a subject that is good |
| 3 | Is good the cause of evil? | Accidentally, yes |
| 4 | Is evil adequately divided into guilt and penalty? | Yes |
| 5 | Is penalty or guilt the greater evil? | Guilt |

**Key passage** (DM 1.1 corp.):
> "Evil is not something, but is the privation of something, namely of good."

**Key passage** (DM 1.2 corp.):
> "Evil cannot exist except in good as its subject."

| Article | Question |

| 12 | Does the devil cause sin? |

#### Q. 6: Human Choice

A famous single-article question on free will and sin.

**Key passage** (DM 6 corp.):

## Research Tips

1. **Detailed arguments**: Disputed Questions often give fuller philosophical development than the *Summa*
2. **Date matters**: Compare earlier (De Veritate) with later (ST) to track development
3. **Against opponents**: Many articles respond to specific philosophical positions
4. **Objections are real**: Unlike *Summa* where some objections seem constructed, DQ objections often reflect actual debates
5. **Cross-reference**: Same topics appear in multiple works—compare treatments
6. **Philosophical depth**: These works show Thomas the philosopher at his most rigorous
