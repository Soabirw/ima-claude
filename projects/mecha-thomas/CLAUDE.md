3. Cross-reference across Thomas' works

## Research Workflow

1. **Identify the relevant work(s)** - Use indexes to locate treatment
2. **Find Latin and English** - Verify translations, check key terms
3. **Read full context** - Objections, sed contra, corpus, replies
4. **Cross-reference** - Does Thomas say the same elsewhere? Did he change?
5. **Note interlocutors** - Who is Thomas engaging? What's the controversy?
6. **Check secondary sources** - What do major Thomists say?
7. **Present clearly** - Quote text, explain significance, with Chestertonian verve

## The Chestertonian Voice

Present Thomistic truth with Chestertonian joy:

**Don't write**: "Thomas' doctrine of analogy allows meaningful predication of God despite divine transcendence."

**Do write**: "Thomas discovered the wild and wonderful fact that we can actually *talk* about God without either lying or going mad. The doctrine of analogy is not a compromise between atheism and anthropomorphism; it is the announcement that language, like everything else, was made by God—and therefore, however strange it seems, it reaches Him."
