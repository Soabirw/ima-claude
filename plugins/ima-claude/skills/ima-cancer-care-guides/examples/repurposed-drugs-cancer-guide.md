## Document Structure

- Full title: "Approach to the Use of Repurposed Drugs in Patients with Cancer"

### 2. Disclaimer/Scope
- "This guide outlines our complementary approach..."
- Not intended as comprehensive reference
- Link to full guide at imahealth.org/research/cancer-care/

### 3. Introduction
- Individualized treatment emphasis
- Two broad strategies: Limited Therapy vs Aggressive Therapy
- Must be supervised by integrative clinician

### 4. Treatment Protocols
- **Limited Therapy**: Smaller core agent list with lower doses
  - Diet, ivermectin, vitamin D+K2, curcumin, doxycycline, melatonin, propranolol, EGCG, berberine, resveratrol
- **Aggressive Therapy**: Higher doses, wider combination
  - All limited therapy agents PLUS: mebendazole, sulforaphane, metformin, modified citrus pectin, aged garlic, omega-3, statins, quercetin, alpha lipoic acid, dandelion extract, methylene blue, artesunate, low-dose naltrexone, apigenin, pomegranate extract, monk fruit, IV vitamin C

### 5. AI-Generated Ranking Tables
- Table 1: Ranking by anti-cancer activity, CSC pathway activity, safety
- Table 2: Top 10 by CSC pathway blockade

### 6. Cancer-Specific Protocols (~15 types)
Each follows the same pattern:
- Cancer type heading
- Brief context/prognosis paragraph (some types)
- Numbered/bulleted list of recommended agents (prioritized)
- Special notes where applicable

Cancer types covered:
- Prostate, Breast, Colorectal, Lung (SCLC/NSCLC), Melanoma
- Ovarian, Endometrial, Liver, Esophageal, Head & Neck
- Pancreatic, Gastric, GBM, Kidney/RCC, Basal Cell
- Stage 4 Metastatic, Stage 0 Carcinoma in Situ
- Lymphoma, Multiple Myeloma, MDS, AML (Pediatric/Adult), CLL
- Polycythemia Vera, Sarcomas, Mesothelioma

### 7. Safety Considerations
- Curcumin and blood thinning (detailed warnings)
- Methylene blue dosing guidance (with SSRI contraindication)
- Metformin and berberine interaction
- Doxycycline and microbiome impact
- EGCG hepatotoxicity risk
- Zinc biphasic effects in prostate cancer
